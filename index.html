A slower kind of adventure

Follow the trail, stop for a snack, or sleep beneath the sky. Touch or click the wildlife and wait for its response: curious, cautious, playful, or startled. Give it a moment, then try again. You can also use Tab and Enter to interact. The wilderness changes with the hour and season, and its inhabitants keep living when you stop.
