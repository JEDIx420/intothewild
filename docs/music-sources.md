## Attribution

**Forest** by **syncopika**, released under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). [Original work and creator upload](https://opengameart.org/content/forest).

**A Warm Fireplace** and **It’s Getting Freezing Cold** by **MouthlessGames**, from *Snowy Cozyness (Calm Piano/Guitar)*, released under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). [Original works and creator upload](https://opengameart.org/content/snowy-cozyness-calm-pianoguitar).

**Vaporware** by **The Cynic Project** / [cynicmusic.com](https://cynicmusic.com/) / [pixelsphere.org](https://pixelsphere.org/), dedicated under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Original work and creator upload](https://opengameart.org/content/calm-piano-1-vaporware).

Changes for this game: loudness normalization (−18 LUFS integrated / −2 dBTP target), encoding to stereo MP3 at 112 kb/s and 44.1 kHz, and runtime crossfades and mixing with quiet environmental sounds. The complete musical recordings are bundled; no composition is claimed as original to this game. The creators do not endorse the game.

Track selection follows the scenery and time of day. A recording has time to develop before the mood changes; the next recording fades in gently. **Another song** in the field notes changes the selection. Muting pauses the soundtrack at its current position.
